Sodium borohydride is dangerous and flammable, and improper handling can cause fires. Piperidine is a controlled precursor chemical that is heavily regulated by international drug conventions. Dichloromethane is a hazardous solvent with known carcinogenic risk. Fentanyl's extreme potency means even a microgram-scale dose can be lethal, which is why it is so dangerous.
